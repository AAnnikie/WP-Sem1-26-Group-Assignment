About Titchfield High School

School History

Since 1786, Titchfield High School has been a beacon of academic excellence, character development, and community leadership in Port Antonio, Jamaica. Located at 22 Fort George Street on the historic site of the former Fort George barracks.

Outstanding academic performance

Average pass rates of 85% CSEC and 88% CAPE over recent years

Sports Champions

First and only school to win back-to-back Inter-Secondary girls' titles (1963-64)

Quiz Excellence

Two-time TVJ Schools' Challenge Quiz champions (2016, 2025) - defeating Jamaica College in 2025.

Historic Legacy

Fifth-oldest high school in Jamaica, established 1786
